WHAT “100%” MEANS HERE

Exact file delivery on your own site.

In Preserve Master mode, FrameForge never re-encodes the video stream. The server verifies the encoded stream and then serves the stored MP4 directly using HTTP byte ranges. People opening your FrameForge share page receive that stored file without FrameForge recompressing it.

A browser, display, device, HDR pipeline, or third-party social network can still render or transcode it differently. That part is outside this website's control.
